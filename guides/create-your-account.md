# Create Your Account

## Before you begin
You will need the invitation email your administrator sent you. The link in it expires after 7 days — if yours has expired, ask your administrator to resend it.

## Steps
1. Open the invitation email and select **Accept Invitation**.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Create a password. It must be at least 12 characters and include a number.
4. Select **Create Account**.
5. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and select the link in it.

## You're done when
You can sign in and see your name in the top-right corner of the screen.

## If something goes wrong
- **No invitation email?** Check your spam folder, then ask your administrator to confirm the email address they used.
- **Password rejected?** Add characters until the strength bar turns green.
