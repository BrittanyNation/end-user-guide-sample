# View and Download Your Invoices

## Steps
1. Sign in and go to **Settings → Billing**.
2. Find the invoice in the **History** table.
3. Select **Download PDF**.

## Update your payment method
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Payment Method**.
2. Select **Replace Card** and enter the new details.
3. Select **Save**. The new card is charged from the next billing cycle.

## You're done when
The new card shows under **Payment Method** with the correct last four digits.

## Common questions
- **Why is this charge higher than last month?** Seat changes are prorated — the invoice PDF lists each line.
- **Can I get invoices by email?** Yes. Turn on **Email invoices** under **Settings → Billing**.
